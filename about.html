📖 Our Story

Who We Are

GMAS (Global Management and Accounting Solutions) is a professional services firm based in Birtamode-04, Jhapa, Nepal. We specialize in accounting, auditing, tax compliance, business consulting, and professional training.

Our mission is to make professional financial services accessible to every business in Nepal, regardless of size — and to develop the next generation of accounting professionals through quality training.

We operate on the principles of accuracy, integrity, and affordability — delivering global-standard work from a local office you can actually walk into.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide affordable, accurate, and professional accounting and management services that empower businesses and individuals to achieve financial clarity and growth.

Our Vision

To be the leading accounting and management consultancy in Eastern Nepal, recognized for quality, integrity, and commitment to building a financially literate community.
