❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with businesses outside Jhapa?+
Yes! We serve clients across Nepal. Documents can be submitted digitally and we communicate via phone, email, and WhatsApp.
How do I get started with your accounting services?+
Simply fill out our enquiry form or call us directly. We'll schedule a free consultation to understand your needs and provide a customized proposal.
What accounting software do you use?+
We work with Tally ERP, QuickBooks, Excel-based systems, and custom ledger formats. We adapt to your existing systems or recommend the best fit.
Are your audit reports accepted by IRD?+
Yes. Our audit reports are prepared by certified professionals and are fully compliant with IRD and Nepal Companies Act requirements.
Can you help with VAT registration for a new business?+
Absolutely. We handle the complete VAT and PAN registration process — from documentation to submission at IRD.
